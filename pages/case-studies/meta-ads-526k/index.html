A full-funnel Meta Ads architecture built from audience temperature — top-of-funnel warming, mid-funnel retargeting, and bottom-of-funnel conversion — delivering a 12.67x return on every dollar spent.

The Challenge

The client — an ecommerce brand — was running ads but not generating a profitable return. Their existing campaigns were broad, poorly structured, and spending budget without a clear funnel strategy. Every dollar in ad spend was either breaking even or losing money. They needed a complete rebuild.

The Objective

Build a Meta Ads system that generates consistent, profitable purchases at a target ROAS above 5x. The secondary objective was to generate enough purchase data to enable algorithmic optimisation — getting Meta's AI working properly requires volume, and the campaign structure needed to enable that.

The Strategy

The campaign was rebuilt around audience temperature. Cold audiences needed warming before being asked to buy. Warm audiences needed retargeting with specific product reminders. Hot audiences — cart abandoners and past purchasers — needed conversion-focused messaging with urgency.

Three campaign layers ran simultaneously: top-of-funnel engagement campaigns at low cost per action building awareness and pixel data, mid-funnel retargeting campaigns showing products to people who had visited but not purchased, and bottom-of-funnel conversion campaigns targeting the warmest audiences with direct purchase CTAs and dynamic product ads.

The Execution

Multiple ad sets within each campaign layer tested different audience definitions — broad interests, lookalike audiences built from customer lists, and website custom audiences at different timeframes. Highest Volume bidding was used to let the algorithm find buyers efficiently rather than constraining it with manual bids early in the campaign.

Creative was rotated regularly based on CTR and hook rate data. Ad sets showing below-target cost per purchase were paused and budget reallocated to the performers. The $26.63 cost per purchase ad set was identified as the clearest winner and scaled first.

The campaign achieved a 12.67x ROAS — meaning every dollar invested in advertising returned $12.67 in revenue. At 12,158 purchases with an average cost per purchase of $3.42, the campaign was operating in the top 1% of Meta Ads performance for ecommerce.

The Key Lesson

Most ecommerce brands run one campaign to one audience and wonder why results are inconsistent. The key to this result was treating cold, warm, and hot audiences as completely different buying stages — each with different messaging, different creative, and different objectives. When you match the ad to where the person is in their decision journey, cost per purchase drops dramatically.
